Parameters & data notes
spot
A configured place ID from /api/v1/spots. Leave out for all places.
date
Defaults to tomorrow in Pacific/Auckland. Also accepts today or YYYY-MM-DD, up to six days ahead.
surf_factor
Optional multiplier from 0.1 to 3. Requires a spot. Only changes the surf estimate; the raw swell stays available.
offshore_from
Optional offshore wind bearing, 0–359°. Requires a spot.

Units: metres, seconds, km/h, °C and millimetres. Directions describe where wind and swell come from. Rain is the preceding hour’s accumulation. Missing values are null. Check warnings and stale; combined responses also include per-spot errors. Forecasts are cached for one hour, with explicitly marked fallback data for up to 6 hours.
