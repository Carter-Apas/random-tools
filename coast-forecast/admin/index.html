How the point model works

Each enabled curve or surface turns its inputs into signed points. We add the earned points, divide by the sum of every enabled curve or surface maximum, then multiply by 100 and clamp the result to 0–100. A function with a larger positive maximum has more influence. Negative points cancel positive ones; a large negative penalty can make the whole day a zero. A wind surface contributes its highest point once and replaces separate speed/direction points.

Smooth curves stay inside their control-point values, so the displayed maximum is the true maximum. Linear and step functions are also available. Outside a curve’s control-point range, its endpoint value is held. Direction curves wrap at north. Disabled variables are ignored, including their maxima. An enabled curve with missing input makes the score unavailable.
