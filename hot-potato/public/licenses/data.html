Data credits

Hot Potato combines open reference data with reviewed names and classifications.

  • Country and capital data: mledoze/countries, adapted under the Open Database License 1.0.
  • Unit names and quantity kinds: QUDT, adapted under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. SI prefixes follow the BIPM.
  • Names and classifications: Wikidata, under CC0.
  • Element data: NIH PubChem. Constellation names: NASA StarChild.
  • Everyday object vocabulary: LVIS v1, by Agrim Gupta, Piotr Dollár and Ross Girshick, under CC BY 4.0. Names and synonyms are adapted from the Detectron2 distribution; Apache 2.0 notice. Context tags and regional aliases are Hot Potato additions. Images are not included.
  • Vehicle makes, models and specifications: FuelEconomy.gov / US EPA public data, combined with global Wikidata vehicle names and reviewed classifications.
  • Pokémon names and properties: PokeAPI, under the BSD 3-Clause license. English species names, default-form types and generation properties are adapted for the questions.
  • Job titles and classifications: the O*NET® 31.0 Database, U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration (USDOL/ETA), under CC BY 4.0. Hot Potato adapts titles into answer aliases and question filters. USDOL/ETA has not approved, endorsed or tested these modifications. O*NET® is a trademark of USDOL/ETA.
  • Spelling word list: Sindre Sorhus's word-list, under the MIT license.

The source code and offline data snapshots include the adaptations, provenance and reviewed corrections.
