The maths behind the spool

A reel holds a fixed volume of line. As diameter increases, the length that fits decreases with the square of that diameter.

New length = known length × (known diameter ÷ new diameter)²

PE is a fibre density rating, not an exact diameter. PE inputs use the VARIVAS approximate PE diameter chart (PE 0.1–30). Sizes between entries are estimated by interpolating cross-sectional area.

Use the braid’s stated diameter when available. Braid construction, coating, winding tension and backing affect actual capacity. Estimates assume the same fill level and no change in backing.

1 yard = 0.9144 metres. Results are rounded to the nearest metre or yard; calculations retain full precision.
